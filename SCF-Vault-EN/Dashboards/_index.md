## ⭐ Dual Score Model

The vault uses **two separate scores**:

| Score | Question | Dashboard |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **🚀 Implementation** | Is the control installed? | [[Dashboards/Implementation Dashboard\|Implementation]] |
| **✅ Compliance** | Does it meet the standard? | [[Dashboards/Compliance Dashboard\|Compliance]] |

> **Workflow:** Planned → In Progress → Completed → **Validated** → Compliance Assessment
